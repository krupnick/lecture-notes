1. Setting the stage

On the face of it, we know a lot.

Someone give me some easy examples of things that you know.

I know that CA is a state; that Sacramento is the capital; that the GSW are a basketball; that dinosaurs once existed; that there will be some airplane flights from SFO to Newark later today; and so on.

Yet there are also some seemingly very compelling reasons for saying the opposite---that we know essentially nothing about the world.

What are these seemingly compelling reasons for saying that we don’t know anything?

You probably have encountered them

Philosophers are trying to figure out what is going on here.
